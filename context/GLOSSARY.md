# Glossary

- Property: a real-estate asset or parcel that can be analyzed in context.
- Geographic Area: a region such as a census tract, county, metro area, or state.
- Census Tract: a small geographic area defined by the U.S. Census Bureau.
- Census Block Group: a subdivision of a census tract.
- Metro Area: a metropolitan statistical area used for regional comparison.
- Metric: a measurable concept such as population or median rent.
- Observation: a recorded measurement for a metric at a point in time and geography.
- Provider: an upstream source of raw data.
- Level: the absolute value of a metric.
- Trend: the change in a metric over time.
- Relative Metric: a metric expressed in relation to a benchmark such as the metro average.
- Risk: a measurement or signal that indicates downside exposure or uncertainty.
- Market Liquidity: a future analytical concept representing the ease of buying and selling an asset.
- Housing Supply Pressure: a future analytical concept representing the balance between demand and available inventory.
- Employment Concentration: a future analytical concept representing how concentrated jobs are in an area.
- Employment Accessibility: a future analytical concept representing how easily residents can reach jobs.
- Catalyst: a structured event that may influence future market conditions.
