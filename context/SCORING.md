# Scoring

Derived scores are deferred to later iterations. When they are introduced, they should expose their underlying inputs, formulas, weights, freshness, and missing-input state so they remain explainable.

Potential future scores include Neighborhood Momentum Score, Economic Resilience Score, Employment Access Score, Housing Supply Pressure, Market Liquidity Score, Education Trend Score, Risk Score, and Relative Valuation Score.

Scores should not be treated as opaque or universal; different users and strategies value different factors.
