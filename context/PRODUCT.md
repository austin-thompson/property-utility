## Problem Statement

Real-estate information is fragmented across property datasets, Census data, employment datasets, housing-market datasets, education datasets, government datasets, transportation systems, environmental-risk systems, and municipal datasets. Analyzing a property or market often requires manually correlating these independent sources.

Property Utility should normalize these sources into a consistent geographic intelligence model.

## Target Users

Initially, the project targets individual analysts, developers, technically sophisticated home buyers, real-estate investors, and market researchers. It is not optimized for traditional real-estate-agent workflows.

## Core Use Cases

- property contextual analysis
- area analysis
- market comparison
- trend analysis
- geographic ranking
- opportunity screening
- risk screening

## Product Principles

- Facts before scores.
- Trends matter more than snapshots.
- Geographic context matters.
- Provider-specific schemas must not become domain models.
- Analytical conclusions must remain traceable to underlying measurements.
- Relative measurements are often more meaningful than absolute measurements.
- Historical data should remain reproducible.
- Complexity must be justified by actual requirements.

## Non-goals

- MLS replacement
- property listing marketplace
- mortgage origination
- automated real-estate transactions
- property-management platform
- public multi-tenant SaaS
- frontend-heavy experience
- opaque AI recommendations
